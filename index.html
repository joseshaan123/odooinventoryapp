Experience the Odoo Inventory app

Try it yourself in three simple steps. First, click the purple Forecast Demand button and Odoo will estimate how fast each product will sell. Each row then shows how many days of stock are left and whether it is time to reorder: red means order now, amber means order soon, and green means you are fine. Next, click any product to see its sales chart and the predicted demand, and press Order to create a purchase order in one click. Finally, tick Seasonal peak to see how a busy season changes the advice. This is a demonstration with sample data, so nothing you do here is saved.
